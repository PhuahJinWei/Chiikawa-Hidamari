# ちいかわと ひだまり

*Chiikawa and the sunny spot.*

A tiny planet, small enough to walk around, with three friends living on it —
and you live there too. You are Momonga: you arrive on the doorstep of the
house at their height, in their world, and from there the day is yours.

Nobody is in danger. Nothing is timed. Nothing needs beating. This is a place
to visit, not a game to win.

Non-commercial fan work. Every drawing in it is original — no official assets
are used or redistributed.

## The world

The whole planet is a few minutes' stroll around. A house on a hill sits at
the middle of the map, with a giant pudding cup and a few big trees for
landmarks and worn tracks between them. Ponds hold twelve kinds of fish.
Mushrooms come up under the trees, weeds grow where weeds grow.

The day passes on its own — morning light, evening lamps, and after midnight
everyone walks home, puts their own lights out, and sleeps. Weather drifts
through: rain, snow that buries the meadow deep enough to hold your
footprints, a rainbow when it clears. When the cold sets in, the ponds freeze
over and you can walk out onto the ice.

Pinch outward and you lift off — the view pulls back until you're looking at
the whole planet from space, which is also the map: spot a friend on the far
side and tap to go to them. Pinch again to set back down.

## Your friends

Three of them, and they are three people rather than a preference system:

- **Chiikawa** — quietly floored by a fistful of weeds.
- **Hachiware** — wants to know what *kind* of fish it is.
- **Usagi** — has strong feelings about mushrooms and no way to explain them.

They roam the whole planet on their own errands, talk to you one-sidedly, and
notice you when you come to see them. Hop near somebody and after a beat they
hop back — two bounces, the second smaller, the way a small creature is
pleased. Take someone's hand and they'll walk with you. At night they go home
to their own beds — except Usagi, who sleeps outside, because of course Usagi
sleeps outside.

## Things to do

- **Wander.** Tap the ground to stroll somewhere, or push a thumb-stick to
  walk. Double-tap to hop. Walk into a stump or a table and hop, and you
  climb onto it.
- **Fish.** Cast from any shore, strike when the float dips, and walk your
  catch over to somebody — the natural next thing to do with a fresh fish
  here.
- **Pick things.** Weeds (草むしり is its own small pleasure), mushrooms,
  and whatever else the ground offers. A sixteen-slot backpack fills faster
  than you'd think.
- **Fill the 図鑑.** Every kind of fish and find gets a page; silhouettes
  mark the ones you haven't met yet.
- **Give gifts.** Anything in the pack can be handed over. Everyone is
  pleased; the right thing for the right person is an event.
- **Keep house.** Lamps switch on and off by hand, a carried lantern brings
  its light with it, and Chiikawa's plushie bear can be nudged along the
  floor with your feet — the one thing in the world your walking moves.
- **Or nothing at all.** Sit on a stump, watch the snow come down, and let
  somebody wander over to you for a change.

## Playing it

Mobile only, portrait, Japanese only. It's a plain web page — no install, no
account — and it works offline after the first visit.

To run it from a copy of this repository:

```bash
python serve.py
```

then open `http://localhost:8099` on a phone, or in a browser's phone
emulation.
